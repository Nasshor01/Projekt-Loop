Tired of classic deckbuilders with nothing extra?
Tired of staring at characters that look like they forgot to be women?

Play Projekt LOOP.
(Yes — Projekt LOOP is the full title. Not “a project”. The game.)

It’s the deckbuilder you already know, smashed together with a combat grid in the spirit of Heroes of Might and Magic III. You play anime girls in a dark fantasy world — properly feminine, blood-and-milk gorgeous, big tits, and still rough as hell.

Build a deck. Hunt the right artifacts. Break the game into nonsense.

What you do

Pick a character. Walk a procedurally generated branching map (the STS-style node map: fights, elites, events, shop, treasure, and the act boss at the end). Every run should feel different — more enemies on a node means more problems.

Watch the RNG. It will happily ruin your “perfect” run. That’s part of the joke. Everything is possible here. Perfect is just rare as shit.

Why the grid?

Because I didn’t want another stand-still card game. I wanted cards and tactics — initiative instead of a boring you-go / they-go stare-down. Terrain matters. You don’t always need a block card to survive a hit — just walk away, lol — or knock the bastard to his knees.

Starter character is simple on purpose so you learn the basics. Once cards and artifacts start stacking, you suddenly care where you stand. Later characters (Trapper first) are built to abuse the grid for real.

Lore (scraps)

You won’t get a clean story dump. Stitch the scraps yourself. Here’s what’s certain:

The village never stops. It keeps sending better and better girls out — stronger than the last ones. Nobody has ever come back. So they don’t stop sending them. You can’t walk back into the village. One-way trip.

On the road you gather materials to slowly upgrade the village and maybe figure out why return is impossible. Nobody knows what happens when a girl dies out there — or why she still doesn’t come home after the final boss.

Why girls? Because those bodies can carry power that holds up against what’s beyond the village walls.

Playable now

Paladin — one of the first lines sent out of the village. She prays to the fallen and fights undead and demonic shit outside. She carries holy power (cards tagged HOLY — bonus damage vs those enemies). Classic tank: walk forward, kill them. Not much fancy positioning required. Good place to learn the game.

Trapper — lays traps, baits enemies into them, more grid-brain required. Also has ranged cards with poison. Fun — and still WIP (small card set), so you probably won’t reach the boss.

Challenge: somehow reach the boss with Trapper, send me a screenshot, and I’ll put your name in the credits.

Key stuff

  • Game-breaking deckbuilder + artifacts — build the pile, find the cursed toys that make runs stupid.
  • Grid position — chess-board brain. Move, then think, then play.
  • Initiative combat — higher initiative = you act earlier next round. Cards can mess with it.
  • Village meta — Loop Hero vibes: send materials home, upgrade buildings, get stronger between trips. No giant skill tree. I tried. It was too much. Village won.
  • More characters coming — a bunch of unique girls planned over time (sketch pile is real). Each one should play the grid differently. Same core: build the deck.

Current Status — Pre-Alpha 0.3.9

Bugs will still appear — this is still Pre-Alpha.

Important Notes

Card readability
I’m still fighting fonts / sizes / scaling. Biggest pain point right now.
Right-click any card for a full tooltip if the text is hard to read.

Controls (short)
• Play card → Drag onto target/tile or click card then click target
• Card tooltip → Right-click
• Move → Click yourself → click a green tile
• Battle camera → WASD pan, mouse wheel zoom, hold middle mouse / Q-E to rotate
• Map pan → Mouse wheel or hold middle/right mouse
• End Turn → Button in UI
